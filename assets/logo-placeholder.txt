CloudXcelAI Logo Placeholder

To add your logo:
1. Save your logo image as "logo.png" in this folder (assets/)
2. The logo should be the CloudXcel.AI circular logo with:
   - White "CloudXcel" text
   - Blue ".AI" text
   - Dark/black circular background
3. Recommended size: 200x200 pixels or similar
4. Format: PNG with transparent background (recommended)

Once added, the logo will automatically appear in:
- Main application header (top of page)
- Sidebar navigation

The application will automatically detect and display the logo when present.
If no logo is found, text-only branding will be displayed.
